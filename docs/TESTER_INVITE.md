**What to know before you start**

- **Your records stay on your phone.** There's no account and no sign-in.
  Transactions, balances and plans are saved only on your device. FireBuddy
  never keeps a copy, so if you delete the app or reset your phone, they're
  gone unless you saved a backup (Profile → Back up to a file).
- **DouDou, the AI guide, is optional and off until you turn it on.** When it's
  on, your question is sent with exact amounts and your account names removed,
  plus only the rough figures that question needs. Nothing is used to train AI.
- **It's not financial advice.** FireBuddy and DouDou give general
  information and estimates only. Please check important decisions with
  official sources (CPF, IRAS, MoneySense) or a licensed adviser.
- **Test builds can change or break.** Features may change between builds, and
  a new build might ask you to start fresh. Keep a backup of anything you'd
  miss.

By installing and using FireBuddy you agree to the Terms of use and Privacy
policy, which you can read in the app under Profile → About.
