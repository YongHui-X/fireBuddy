Keeping an emergency buffer in Singapore Savings Bonds (SSBs) rather than immediately accessible cash involves several liquidity trade-offs:

- SSBs can be redeemed in any month without penalty, allowing you to access your capital when needed.
- However, SSBs cannot be traded or pledged as collateral, limiting their liquidity compared to cash.
- While SSBs provide step-up interest and are backed by the government, they may not be as immediately accessible as cash in a bank account.

- Each redemption incurs a $2 transaction fee, which may affect the overall liquidity if frequent access is needed. 

In summary, while SSBs offer a secure and interest-earning option for emergency funds, they come with restrictions that may impact immediate access compared to cash.
