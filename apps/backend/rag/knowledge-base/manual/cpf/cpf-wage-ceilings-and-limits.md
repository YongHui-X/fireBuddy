# CPF wage ceilings and the CPF Annual Limit

Three separate caps limit how much CPF can be contributed: a monthly ceiling on
Ordinary Wages, an annual ceiling on Additional Wages, and an overall cap on
total contributions for the calendar year.

## The three caps

| Cap | 2026 value | What it limits |
|---|---|---|
| Ordinary Wage (OW) ceiling | $8,000 per month | Monthly salary subject to CPF contributions |
| Additional Wage (AW) ceiling | $102,000 minus total Ordinary Wages subject to CPF for the year | Bonuses and other additional wages subject to CPF for the year |
| CPF Annual Limit | $37,740 | Total mandatory and voluntary CPF contributions in a calendar year |

## How the Additional Wage ceiling works

The AW ceiling is computed per employer, per year, using this formula:

$102,000 minus total Ordinary Wages subject to CPF for the year.

For 2026, an employee whose monthly wage is at or above the $8,000 OW ceiling
for all twelve months has total OW subject to CPF of $8,000 x 12 = $96,000, so
the AW ceiling is $102,000 - $96,000 = $6,000.

The applicable AW ceiling depends on when the additional wage is due and
payable, not when it was earned. A 2025 bonus paid in 2026 uses the 2026 AW
ceiling.

## Quick answers

- The Ordinary Wage ceiling is $8,000 a month in 2026.
- The Additional Wage ceiling for 2026 is $102,000 minus the total Ordinary
  Wages subject to CPF for the year.
- An employee earning $8,000 a month or more for the whole of 2026 has an AW
  ceiling of $6,000.
- The CPF Annual Limit is $37,740 and covers mandatory and voluntary
  contributions together.
- The OW ceiling and the AW ceiling are separate caps; the AW ceiling is not
  reduced below zero.
