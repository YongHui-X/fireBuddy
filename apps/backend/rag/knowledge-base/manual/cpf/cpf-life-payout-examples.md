# CPF LIFE payout examples: savings needed at 55, 60 and 65

CPF LIFE pays a monthly income for life from age 65. The table shows how much
a member needs in the Retirement Account at 55, 60 or 65 to receive a given
monthly payout: for example, $213,000 set aside at 55 (the 2025 Full
Retirement Sum) supports an estimated $1,610 to $1,730 a month. The figures
are estimates for the CPF LIFE Standard Plan for members who turn 65 in 2035,
computed as of 2025.

## Payout table

| Desired monthly payout from 65 | CPF LIFE premium at 65 (savings needed at 65) | Savings needed at 60 | Savings needed at 55 |
|---|---|---|---|
| $540 to $570 | $97,300 | $75,900 | $60,000 |
| $860 to $930 | $164,800 | $131,400 | $106,500 |
| $1,170 to $1,250 | $227,900 | $183,300 | $150,000 |
| $1,610 to $1,730 | $319,400 | $258,500 | $213,000 |
| $3,100 to $3,330 | $628,600 | $512,700 | $426,000 |

## How to read the table

- To receive a monthly payout of $1,610 to $1,730 from age 65, a member needs
  $319,400 in the Retirement Account at 65. A much smaller sum of $213,000 is
  enough if it is set aside in the Retirement Account at 55, because CPF
  interest of up to 6% per annum compounds over the ten years in between.
- The $213,000 at 55 row corresponds to the 2025 Full Retirement Sum, the
  $106,500 row to the 2025 Basic Retirement Sum, and the $426,000 row to the
  2025 Enhanced Retirement Sum.
- The interest assumption is based on the current 4% per annum floor on
  Retirement Account monies.
- Payouts may be adjusted for long-term changes in interest rates or life
  expectancy. Such adjustments, if any, are expected to be small and gradual.
- These are estimates for the Standard Plan. The Basic Plan and Escalating
  Plan produce different payout profiles for the same premium.
