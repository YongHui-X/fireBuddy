# CPF Basic Healthcare Sum (BHS) by year

The Basic Healthcare Sum is the maximum amount that can be held in the MediSave
Account. It is raised each year for members below 65. Once a member turns 65
their BHS is fixed for the rest of their life at the value for the year they
turned 65.

## Basic Healthcare Sum by cohort year

| Year the member turns 65 | Basic Healthcare Sum |
|---|---|
| 2016 or earlier | $49,800 |
| 2017 | $52,000 |
| 2018 | $54,500 |
| 2019 | $57,200 |
| 2020 | $60,000 |
| 2021 | $63,000 |
| 2022 | $66,000 |
| 2023 | $68,500 |
| 2024 | $71,500 |
| 2025 | $75,500 |
| 2026 | $79,000 |

## Quick answers

- The BHS for 2026 is $79,000. It applies to members below 65 and is fixed for
  life for members who turn 65 in 2026.
- The BHS for 2025 was $75,500 and for 2024 was $71,500.
- Members who turned 65 in 2016 or earlier have a BHS of $49,800 for life.
- MediSave savings above the BHS overflow to the Special Account before 55, or
  to the Retirement Account from 55, and cannot be taken in cash on that basis
  alone.
- The BHS is the cap for MediSave top-ups that qualify for tax relief.
