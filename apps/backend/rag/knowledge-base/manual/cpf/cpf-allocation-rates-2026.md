# CPF allocation rates from 1 January 2026

Each dollar of CPF contribution is split across the member's accounts by age.
For a member aged 35 and below, 62.17% goes to the Ordinary Account, 16.21% to
the Special Account and 21.62% to the MediSave Account. The MediSave share
rises with age and, above 55, the Special Account share becomes a Retirement
Account share. The ratios apply to private sector and non-pensionable
employees of ministries, statutory bodies and aided schools.

## Allocation ratios by age

For members aged 55 and below, contributions are split across the Ordinary
Account (OA), Special Account (SA) and MediSave Account (MA):

| Employee age | Ordinary Account (ratio) | Special Account (ratio) | MediSave Account (ratio) |
|---|---|---|---|
| 35 and below | 0.6217 (62.17%) | 0.1621 (16.21%) | 0.2162 (21.62%) |
| Above 35 to 45 | 0.5677 (56.77%) | 0.1891 (18.91%) | 0.2432 (24.32%) |
| Above 45 to 50 | 0.5136 (51.36%) | 0.2162 (21.62%) | 0.2702 (27.02%) |
| Above 50 to 55 | 0.4055 (40.55%) | 0.3108 (31.08%) | 0.2837 (28.37%) |

For members above 55, the Special Account is closed and the middle column is the
Retirement Account (RA):

| Employee age | Ordinary Account (ratio) | Retirement Account (ratio) | MediSave Account (ratio) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Above 55 to 60 | 0.3530 (35.30%) | 0.3382 (33.82%) | 0.3088 (30.88%) |
| Above 60 to 65 | 0.1400 (14.00%) | 0.4400 (44.00%) | 0.4200 (42.00%) |
| Above 65 to 70 | 0.0607 (6.07%) | 0.3030 (30.30%) | 0.6363 (63.63%) |
| Above 70 | 0.0800 (8.00%) | 0.0800 (8.00%) | 0.8400 (84.00%) |

## Notes on the allocation

- Upon the closure of the Special Account, contributions for members aged 55
  and above are fully allocated to the Retirement Account up to the Full
  Retirement Sum (FRS). If a member has already set aside the FRS in the RA,
  those contributions are channelled to the Ordinary Account instead.
- The allocation is computed first for the MediSave Account, then for the
  Special or Retirement Account. The remainder goes to the Ordinary Account.
  This is why the OA amount in the examples is worked out by subtraction.
- Younger members receive a larger OA share, which supports housing, education
  and CPF Investment Scheme use. Older members receive a larger MediSave share
  for healthcare needs.

## Example 1: employee aged 30 with a $100 contribution

| Age band | CPF contribution | Ordinary Account | Special Account | MediSave Account |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 35 and below | $100 | $62.17 ($100 - $21.62 - $16.21) | $16.21 ($100 x 0.1621) | $21.62 ($100 x 0.2162) |

## Example 2: employee aged 57 with a $100 contribution

| Age band | CPF contribution | Ordinary Account | Retirement Account | MediSave Account |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Above 55 to 60 | $100 | $35.30 ($100 - $33.82 - $30.88) | $33.82 ($100 x 0.3382) | $30.88 ($100 x 0.3088) |
