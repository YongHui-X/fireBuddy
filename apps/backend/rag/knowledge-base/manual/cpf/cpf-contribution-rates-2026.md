# CPF contribution rates from 1 January 2026

From 1 January 2026, CPF contribution rates for Singapore Citizens and
Permanent Residents (from the third year of PR status) earning more than $750
a month are set by employee age. The rates apply to Ordinary Wages up to the
$8,000 monthly ceiling and to Additional Wages, for private sector and
non-pensionable employees of ministries, statutory bodies and aided schools:

| Employee age | Total CPF contribution | Employee's share | Employer's share |
|---|---|---|---|
| 55 and below | 37% | 20% | 17% |
| Above 55 to 60 | 34% | 18% | 16% |
| Above 60 to 65 | 25% | 12.5% | 12.5% |
| Above 65 to 70 | 16.5% | 7.5% | 9% |
| Above 70 | 12.5% | 5% | 7.5% |

Lower wage bands, first and second year PR rates, maximum contributions at the
ceiling and the computation rules follow in Tables 1 to 5.

Definitions used in every table:

- OW: Ordinary Wages, capped at the OW ceiling of $8,000 per month.
- AW: Additional Wages, such as bonuses.
- TW: Total Wages for the calendar month, equal to OW plus AW.
- Total CPF contribution: the employer's share plus the employee's share.
- Employer's share: total contribution minus the employee's share.
- Max: the maximum monthly contribution on OW at the OW ceiling.

## Table 1: Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents from the 3rd year (full rates)

Full employer and full employee rates for Singapore Citizens, and for Singapore
Permanent Residents from the third year of SPR status. These rates also apply to
first and second year SPR employees whose employer and employee jointly applied
to contribute at full rates.

Summary for employees earning more than $750 a month (rates applied to OW up to
the $8,000 ceiling and to AW):

| Employee age | Total CPF contribution | Employee's share | Employer's share | Max total on OW | Max employee on OW |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| 55 and below | 37% | 20% | 17% | $2,960 | $1,600 |
| Above 55 to 60 | 34% | 18% | 16% | $2,720 | $1,440 |
| Above 60 to 65 | 25% | 12.5% | 12.5% | $2,000 | $1,000 |
| Above 65 to 70 | 16.5% | 7.5% | 9% | $1,320 | $600 |
| Above 70 | 12.5% | 5% | 7.5% | $1,000 | $400 |

Lower wage bands for Table 1 (employee's share is nil at $500 and below, and
phases in between $500 and $750):

| Employee age | Total wages $50 or less | Total wages above $50 to $500 | Total wages above $500 to $750 (total) | Total wages above $500 to $750 (employee's share) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| 55 and below | Nil | 17% of TW | 17% of TW + 0.6 x (TW - $500) | 0.6 x (TW - $500) |
| Above 55 to 60 | Nil | 16% of TW | 16% of TW + 0.54 x (TW - $500) | 0.54 x (TW - $500) |
| Above 60 to 65 | Nil | 12.5% of TW | 12.5% of TW + 0.375 x (TW - $500) | 0.375 x (TW - $500) |
| Above 65 to 70 | Nil | 9% of TW | 9% of TW + 0.225 x (TW - $500) | 0.225 x (TW - $500) |
| Above 70 | Nil | 7.5% of TW | 7.5% of TW + 0.15 x (TW - $500) | 0.15 x (TW - $500) |

Worked example for Table 1: an employee aged 55 and below earning $5,000 in OW
and no AW in a month has a total CPF contribution of 37% x $5,000 = $1,850, of
which the employee's share is 20% x $5,000 = $1,000 and the employer's share is
$850. At the $8,000 OW ceiling the same employee reaches the maximum of $2,960
total and $1,600 employee share.

| Above 55 to 60 | 9% | 5% | $720 | $400 | 4% of TW | 4% of TW + 0.15 x (TW - $500) |
| Above 60 to 65 | 8.5% | 5% | $680 | $400 | 3.5% of TW | 3.5% of TW + 0.15 x (TW - $500) |
| Above 65 | 8.5% | 5% | $680 | $400 | 3.5% of TW | 3.5% of TW + 0.15 x (TW - $500) |

For wages of $50 or less there is no contribution. For wages above $500 to $750
the employee's share is 0.15 x (TW - $500), and nil at $500 or below. There
have been no changes to the graduated employer and employee rates since
1 January 2016.

## How CPF contributions are computed

Steps published by CPF Board with the rate tables:

1. Compute the total CPF contribution, rounded to the nearest dollar (amounts
   below 50 cents round down, 50 cents and above round up).
2. Compute the employee's share, rounded down to the nearest dollar.
3. Employer's share equals the total contribution minus the employee's share.
4. If only OW is payable in the month, the maximum contribution on OW is rounded
   to the nearest dollar for the total and rounded down for the employee's
   share. If both OW and AW are payable in the same month, compute the
   contributions on OW and on AW separately, sum them, then apply the rounding
   rules.
5. CPF Board provides a CPF contribution calculator on cpf.gov.sg under
   Employer, Tools and services, Calculators.
