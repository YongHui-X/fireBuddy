# CPF retirement sums by year: Basic, Full and Enhanced Retirement Sum

The retirement sum that applies to a CPF member is fixed by the year the
member turns 55 and does not change afterwards. For members turning 55 in
2026 the Basic Retirement Sum (BRS) is $110,200, the Full Retirement Sum (FRS)
is $220,400 and the Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS) is $440,800.

## Definitions

- Basic Retirement Sum (BRS): the amount to set aside in the Retirement Account
  at 55 for a basic level of CPF LIFE monthly payouts, available to members who
  own a property and pledge it.
- Full Retirement Sum (FRS): double the BRS. It is the default amount
  transferred to the Retirement Account at 55 for members without a property
  pledge.
- Enhanced Retirement Sum (ERS): the maximum a member can voluntarily top up
  the Retirement Account to for higher CPF LIFE payouts. Up to 2024 the ERS was
  3 times the BRS. From 1 January 2025 the ERS was raised to 2 times the FRS
  (4 times the BRS).

For members turning 55 in 2026 the three sums are: BRS $110,200, FRS $220,400
and ERS $440,800. The full year-by-year table follows.

2024 values are 3 times the BRS, the rule that applied in those years. 2025 is
2 times the FRS under the rule that started on 1 January 2025 and matches the
$426,000 figure in the CPF LIFE payout examples. 2026 and 2027 are published by
CPF Board.

## Quick answers

- In 2026 the BRS is $110,200, the FRS is $220,400 and the ERS is $440,800.
- In 2027 the BRS is $114,100, the FRS is $228,200 and the ERS is $456,400.
- In 2025 the BRS is $106,500, the FRS is $213,000 and the ERS is $426,000.
- The FRS is always 2 times the BRS. From 2025 the ERS is 4 times the BRS.
- Members turning 55 from 1 July 2015 to 2016 had a BRS of $80,500 and an FRS
  of $161,000.
