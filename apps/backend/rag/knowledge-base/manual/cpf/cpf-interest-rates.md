# CPF interest rates: account floor rates and extra interest

CPF savings earn a floor interest rate set by legislation, plus extra interest
on the first $60,000 of combined balances. The floor rates below apply for
1 July to 30 September 2026 and are unchanged from the start of 2026.

## Floor rates by account

| Account | Interest rate per annum |
|---|---|
| Ordinary Account (OA) | 2.5% |
| Special Account (SA) | 4% |
| MediSave Account (MA) | 4% |
| Retirement Account (RA) | 4% |

The OA rate is the legislated minimum of 2.5% per annum. The OA pegged rate is
computed from the 3-month average of the major local banks' interest rates and
has stayed below the floor.

The Special, MediSave and Retirement Account rate is computed from the 12-month
average yield of 10-year Singapore Government Securities plus 1%, subject to
the 4% floor. The Government has extended that 4% floor to 31 December 2026.

The HDB concessionary housing loan rate is 2.6% per annum, which is the OA rate
plus 0.1%.

## Extra interest on combined balances

| Age | Extra interest |
|---|---|
| Below 55 | 1% per annum on the first $60,000 of combined balances, of which at most $20,000 may come from the Ordinary Account |
| 55 and above | 2% per annum on the first $30,000 and 1% per annum on the next $30,000 of combined balances, of which at most $20,000 may come from the Ordinary Account |

Extra interest earned on Ordinary Account savings goes into the Special Account
or, for members aged 55 and above, the Retirement Account. Members on CPF LIFE
continue to receive extra interest on their combined balances.

## Quick answers

- The OA floor rate is 2.5% per annum and the SA, MA and RA floor rate is 4%
  per annum.
- A member below 55 earns up to 5% per annum on the first $60,000 of combined
  balances: the 4% floor plus 1% extra interest.
- A member aged 55 and above earns up to 6% per annum on the first $30,000:
  the 4% floor plus 2% extra interest.
- No more than $20,000 of the Ordinary Account balance counts toward the extra
  interest tiers.
