# IRAS individual income tax reliefs

Tax reliefs reduce chargeable income. The total of all personal income tax
reliefs claimed is capped at $80,000 per Year of Assessment.

This document covers only the reliefs whose current values have been checked
against IRAS; it is not a complete list of every relief available.

## Overall relief cap

| Cap | Amount | Applies |
|---|---|---|
| Personal income tax relief cap | $80,000 | Per Year of Assessment, across all reliefs claimed |

The $80,000 cap applies to the total of all personal income tax reliefs,
including relief on CPF cash top-ups and SRS contributions. Relief above the cap
is not granted and cannot be carried forward.

## Retirement-related reliefs

| Relief | Maximum per year |
|---|---|
| CPF Cash Top-up Relief, own account | $8,000 |
| CPF Cash Top-up Relief, family members' accounts | $8,000 |
| CPF Cash Top-up Relief, combined | $16,000 |
| SRS contributions, Singapore Citizens and Permanent Residents | $15,300 |
| SRS contributions, foreigners | $35,700 |

CPF Cash Top-up Relief applies only to cash top-ups to Special or Retirement
Accounts. Transferring money from your own CPF account to another CPF account
does not qualify. The family-member cap of $8,000 has applied from YA 2023.
Top-ups that attract Matched Retirement Savings Scheme or MediSave matching
grants do not attract this relief.

## Quick answers

- The overall personal income tax relief cap is $80,000 per Year of Assessment.
- CPF cash top-up relief is up to $8,000 for topping up your own account and a
  further $8,000 for topping up family members' accounts, so $16,000 in total.
- The SRS annual contribution cap is $15,300 for Singapore Citizens and
  Permanent Residents and $35,700 for foreigners.
- Reliefs reduce chargeable income; they are not a refund of tax paid.
