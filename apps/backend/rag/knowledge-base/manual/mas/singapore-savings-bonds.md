# Singapore Savings Bonds: product terms

Singapore Savings Bonds (SSB) are issued by the Singapore Government. They pay
step-up interest that rises the longer the bond is held, can be redeemed in any
month without penalty, and cannot lose capital.

## Product terms

| Item | Detail |
|---|---|
| Issuer | Singapore Government |
| Eligibility | Individuals aged 18 and above |
| Term | 10 years |
| Issuance | A new bond every month |
| Minimum investment | $500, and in multiples of $500 thereafter |
| Individual Limit | $200,000 held at any one time, across cash and SRS combined |
| Interest | Paid every 6 months, on the 1st business day of the month |
| Step-up | The rate rises each year the bond is held |
| Redemption | Any month before maturity, with no penalty, in multiples of $500 |
| Redemption proceeds | Principal plus accrued interest, by the 2nd business day of the following month |
| Transaction fee | $2 for each application and $2 for each redemption request |
| Transferable | No, except on death or between an investor's own SRS and CDP accounts |
| Tax | Interest is exempt from tax and is not declared as chargeable income |
| Funding | Cash or SRS funds. CPF funds cannot be used |

## How step-up interest works

Rates are fixed for each monthly issue, based on the average Singapore
Government Securities (SGS) yields of the month before, and locked in for that
issue. Holding a Savings Bond for the full 10 years gives an average return per
year matching the 10-year SGS yield at the point of investment. Redeeming early
gives a lower average return, similar to an SGS of the same tenor.

Occasionally the shape of the SGS yield curve does not allow rates to step up.
MAS then adjusts the schedule so interest payments never step down within the
life of a bond, without changing the return for an investor who holds the full
10 years.

## Quick answers

- The minimum investment is $500, in multiples of $500.
- The Individual Limit is $200,000 at any one time, counting cash and SRS
  holdings together; there is no separate limit for each.
- You can redeem in any month before maturity with no penalty, and receive
  principal plus accrued interest by the 2nd business day of the next month.
- Each application and each redemption costs a $2 transaction fee.
- Interest is paid every 6 months and is exempt from tax.
- SRS funds may be used to buy Savings Bonds. CPF funds may not.
